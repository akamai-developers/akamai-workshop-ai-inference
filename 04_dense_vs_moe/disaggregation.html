Module 4  ·  Look forward: split prefill from decode
Prefill is compute-bound, decode is memory-bound. On one GPU a big prefill stalls everyone's decode. So stop sharing: give each phase its own GPUs and ship the KV cache between them.
Prefill GPU pool
compute-bound
Reads the prompt and the tool schemas, builds the KV cache. Scale for throughput.

Decode GPU pool
memory-bound
Streams the tokens one at a time. Scale for bandwidth, even on cheaper cards.
Not runnable on one 20 GB card. The honest win: a single prefill-plus-decode pair does not raise throughput by itself. The gains come at fleet scale with smart routing, where you size and scale the two phases on their own and a prefill spike never interrupts a decode. vLLM --kv-transfer-config, NVIDIA Dynamo, llm-d.
